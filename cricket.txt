1. Cricket is a bat-and-ball sport played between two teams of 11 players.
2. It originated in England in the 16th century.
3. The game is played on a circular or oval field.
4. The center of the field is called the pitch.
5. The pitch is 22 yards long.
6. One team bats while the other bowls and fields.
7. The main aim is to score more runs than the opponent.
8. A batsman scores runs by hitting the ball and running between wickets.
9. A boundary gives 4 runs if the ball touches the ground before crossing the rope.
10. A six is scored when the ball crosses the boundary without touching the ground.
11. Each team has 10 wickets in an innings.
12. A batsman is out if the bowler hits the stumps (bowled).
13. Catching the ball before it hits the ground also results in an out.
14. LBW (Leg Before Wicket) is another common dismissal.
15. There are three main formats: Test, ODI, and T20.
16. Test matches last up to 5 days.
17. One Day Internationals have 50 overs per side.
18. T20 matches have 20 overs per side.
19. Umpires control the match and make decisions.
20. Cricket is one of the most popular sports in countries like India, Australia, and England. 🏏
